How do you ask?

There are three magical ways to ask the witch — whichever you pick, an answer is waiting.

1) Rub the cauldron

Rub the witch's cauldron with your finger and let a magic scroll bring your answer.

2) Hold the grimoire

Think of your question, hold the Book of Answers and let go; the page it opens to is your answer.

3) Shake or swirl

Shake your phone or swirl the crystal ball's mist with your finger for a quick yes-or-no answer.

4) See your answer

One of 200 answers appears, colored according to its meaning.

How Spinify Works

From idea to result in seconds.

1) Pick or create a wheel

Choose a preset or build slices with emoji and colors.

2) Set weights and options

Fine-tune odds, spin speed, haptics, and nested links.

3) Tap SPIN

Watch the wheel spin with realistic slowdown.

4) Get your result

See the winner with confetti — spin again or open a child wheel.

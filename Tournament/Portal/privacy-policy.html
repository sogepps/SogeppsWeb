🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 February 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application League & Tournament Maker ("App"). By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

a) Personal Information

We do not require users to provide personally identifiable information such as name, address, or phone number.

If you sign in using third-party services (such as Google Sign-In), authentication data is handled securely by the respective provider and is not stored directly by us.

b) Usage Data

We may collect limited, non-personal information automatically, including:

  • App usage statistics
  • Device type and operating system
  • Crash reports and performance data

This data is used solely to improve app stability and user experience.

c) Advertising Data

Our App may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

AdMob may collect and process information such as:

  • Device identifiers
  • Approximate location (country-level)
  • Ad interaction data

This information is used to display ads and measure their performance.

2. Advertisements & Consent

a) User Consent

For users located in regions where consent is required (such as the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland), we request user consent before showing personalized ads.

Users may choose:

  • Personalized ads
  • Non-personalized ads
  • Or decline consent where applicable

Ad serving behavior respects the user's consent choices.

b) Non-Personalized Ads

If consent is not provided, ads may still be shown in a non-personalized manner, in accordance with Google's policies.

3. Credits, Free Usage & Premium Features

The App offers limited free usage through a credit-based system.

Free credits may renew automatically after a defined waiting period.

Optional premium features may be available through in-app purchases.

Watching advertisements is never mandatory to access core functionality.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent user data to third parties.

Limited data may be shared with trusted service providers strictly for:

  • Analytics
  • Advertising
  • App performance monitoring

All such providers comply with applicable data protection regulations.

5. Children's Privacy

Our App is not intended for children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal data through the App, please contact us so we can remove the information.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect user data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure.

7. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services that have their own privacy policies, including but not limited to:

  • Google AdMob
  • Google Analytics for Firebase

We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of these providers for more information.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • Access your data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent for personalized advertising

Requests can be made using the contact information below.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

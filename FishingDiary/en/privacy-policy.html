🔐 Privacy Policy – Fishing Diary: Ocean Legends

Effective date: 5 October 2026

This policy explains how the Fishing Diary: Ocean Legends mobile game ("the Game"), developed by Sogepps, handles data.

In short

  • The game shows ads through Google AdMob: an occasional full-screen ad at natural breaks in play (never in the early game, and never for players who bought something or removed ads), plus optional rewarded videos you can choose to watch.
  • Google AdMob, which serves the ads, may process information as a third party, such as device identifiers (advertising ID), approximate location derived from your IP address, app interaction data and diagnostics, to show ads, limit how often they appear, provide analytics and prevent fraud.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you're asked for consent through Google's consent form (UMP) on first launch; you can change that choice any time in the game's Settings under "Advertising privacy choices". You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings.
  • We (the developer) do not collect or store personal data on our own servers; there's no account, no sign-in and no analytics of our own. Your game progress and settings stay only on your device.
  • Optional in-app purchases (gems, gold packs, the starter pack, ad removal) are processed entirely by Google Play Billing; we never receive your payment card details.
  • The game can be played offline. An internet connection is only used for ads and for purchases through Google Play.

1. We don't collect personal data

We (Sogepps), the developer of Fishing Diary: Ocean Legends, do not collect your personal data, do not store it on our own servers and do not share it with anyone. There is no account or sign-in in the game, and we run no analytics of our own. The game shows ads; see section 3 below for what Google AdMob, as a third party, processes to serve them.

2. Information that stays on your device

Your game progress, settings and purchase records (such as anonymous purchase tokens kept to avoid delivering the same purchase twice) are stored only on your device, in the app's local storage. This information is never sent to us.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK): an occasional full-screen (interstitial) ad at natural breaks in play, and optional rewarded video ads you can choose to watch for extra rewards. To show and measure ads, limit how often you see them, provide analytics and prevent fraud, Google AdMob, as a third party, may collect and process information such as:

  • Device identifiers such as the advertising ID
  • Your IP address and the approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app interaction data (ad impressions and clicks)
  • Diagnostics

How Google uses this data is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and in the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Ad consent and your choices

Players in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown Google's consent form (UMP) on first launch where required and asked for their consent; you can change that choice any time in the game's Settings under "Advertising privacy choices" (shown where applicable).

On any device you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads / Privacy → Ads).

5. In-app purchases (Google Play Billing)

You can optionally buy gems, gold packs, a starter pack or ad removal inside the game. These purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing; we never receive payment details such as card information. To deliver what you bought, the app only receives an anonymous purchase token from Google Play, and it keeps that token on your device to avoid delivering the same purchase twice.

Google's privacy policy applies to payments: policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Internet access

The game can be played offline. An internet connection is only used to show ads and to make or restore purchases through Google Play.

7. Children

Fishing Diary: Ocean Legends is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. Ads are requested with a maximum content rating of PG (a Google AdMob setting).

8. Data deletion

All of your game data stays on your device. Uninstalling the game, or clearing the app's data in your Android settings, permanently deletes your game progress and settings. There is no personal data on our servers to delete. For data processed by Google, such as your advertising ID, you can use the choices in section 4.

9. Changes to this policy

When this policy is updated, the new text is published on this page.

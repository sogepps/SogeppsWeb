🔐 Privacy Policy – Hızlı Çekiliş

Last updated: 2 March 2026

We at Hızlı Çekiliş ("App") value your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use the app, how it is used, and how it is protected.

By using the App, you accept this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Hızlı Çekiliş does not require the collection of personal data. The app runs raffles and random selection without asking for identity information from the user.

Data entered within the app:

  • Name lists
  • Numbers
  • Lists
  • Team information

is used only temporarily on the user's device and is not sent to our servers.

2. Automatically Collected Information

To improve the app's performance and stability, the following anonymous information may be collected automatically:

  • App usage statistics
  • Error and crash reports
  • Device type and operating system information

This data does not contain personally identifiable information and is used only for app development purposes.

3. Advertising

Hızlı Çekiliş may use third-party advertising services (e.g. Google AdMob). These services may collect anonymous data to show more relevant ads to users.

Ad providers are subject to their own privacy policies. Users can disable personalized ads in their device settings.

4. Children's Privacy

Hızlı Çekiliş does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and notice that your child has shared personal information, you may contact us.

5. Data Security

The security of user data is important to us. The app keeps user data on the device as much as possible and takes necessary measures against unauthorized access.

However, no data transmission over the internet or digital environment can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

6. Third-Party Links

The app may contain links to third-party websites or services. Hızlı Çekiliş is not responsible for the privacy practices of these sites. We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of third parties.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page and will be effective from the date of publication.

30+ Fun Games to Play at Home with Friends

HomeParty August 27, 2026·7 min read

The friends have arrived, the snacks are ready, the music is on — and then that familiar silence: "So, what shall we play?" The same story every time. In this article we have collected 30+ games so you never have to ask that question again: party games, classic favorites, brain puzzles and reflex duels. Zero materials, one phone, and everyone gets it in the first round.

Quick note: You can play all of the 30+ games below in the HomeParty app. No box, no deck of cards, no pen and paper — one phone just goes from hand to hand. The app explains the rules, keeps the score itself and shows whose turn is next. It works for 2 to 16 players.

Party Games (14 Games)

Team or solo, loud or silent — the main party games that keep the pace up all night. Pass the phone from hand to hand and the app handles the rest.

1. Fun Series — The star of the night. A 7-round party marathon: voting, drawing, bomb, taboo, dare, charades and who-am-I rounds follow one after another. Every round has a different mechanic and nobody knows what's coming. The mode that ends the "what shall we play?" debate.

2. Silent Word — A word appears on screen and the person explaining can't say a single word. Arms flailing, the team guesses. The game that gets even your shy friends on stage.

3. Silent Cinema — A movie title appears and you act it out with gestures only. Classic charades, but the timer, score and turn order are already handled. In action movies everyone shines, in romances everyone sinks.

4. Who Am I? — You try to work out the famous person on your forehead (really, on the screen). Only yes-or-no questions. Anyone who has ever started with "Am I a man?" knows this game.

5. Story Creation — Take turns adding sentences to build a story together. The longer the chain gets, the more surprises appear: it starts as drama and ends as comedy. A great warm-up for creative groups.

6. Draw Chain — One person writes a word, the next draws it, the next reads the drawing, the next draws that... The longer the chain, the better the nonsense at the end. No drawing skill needed — it's even funnier without.

7. Exploding Word — The word goes from hand to hand and nobody knows when the secret timer will blow. Whoever is holding it when it does is out. Hot potato, but with words. Adrenaline through the roof.

8. Wheel Challenge — Spin the wheel, do the challenge, grab the points. Perfect for a warm-up because there are hardly any rules. Spin, do, laugh, next turn.

9. Taboo — Explain the word without using the forbidden words. The classic of classics, where the loudest person still wins. In team games, arguments are guaranteed.

10. True or False — The next player says a few sentences about themselves, and one is a lie. The group tries to find the lie. The real test of how well you know your friends.

11. Who Is More? — For questions like "Who in this group is late most often?" everyone votes for someone. Seeing who gets the most votes sometimes tests friendships, but on the fun side.

12. Hot Seat — One person sits in the middle and the group fires quick questions. You have to answer, no passing. The star of the night for close-knit groups of friends.

13. Who Is The Agent? — Everyone knows the same location except one person: the agent. You try to find the agent with questions, and the agent tries to work out the location without getting caught. Tailor-made for fans of bluffing and strategy.

14. Most Likely — A question is read out and everyone points at the same time at the person it fits best. Fast, simple and the mood heats up instantly. The favorite warm-up.

How Do You Start?

You open HomeParty, enter the player names, pick a game mode — that's it. The app forms the teams, keeps time, counts the score and shows the next player on screen. You don't even have to explain the rules. In party games, pass the phone from hand to hand; in mini games, everyone joins from their own screen.

5 Tips for a Great Game Night

  • Start lukewarm, finish hot. Make the first game something light like Wheel Challenge, Most Likely or Tombala. Taboo, Who Is The Agent? and Fun Series can join the night later.
  • Play it safe with Fun Series. If you can't decide which game to pick, Fun Series plays 7 different modes back to back. The indecision ends.
  • Mix the genres. Sprinkle a round of Nine Men's Morris or Reflex Duel between the party games — the change of pace keeps the energy high.
  • Get everyone into the game in the first 10 minutes. The person waiting for their turn ends up staring at their phone and tuning out. Mini games let everyone play at the same time.
  • If the group is big, form teams. Once you go past 6 people, team modes (Taboo, Silent Word, Fun Series) keep the pace up.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which games can you play at home with 4 people?

The most fun modes with 4 people: True or False, Draw Chain, Silent Word (in teams of two), Who Am I? and Nine Men's Morris. HomeParty supports all of these modes from 2 players up.

Are there party games that need no materials?

Yes, none of the 30+ games in HomeParty need any materials. All you need is a phone. The app manages rules, time and score automatically.

How many people can play?

HomeParty works for 2 to 16 people. Most party games can be played in teams or solo; brain and mini games are fun even with two.

What's the difference between party games and mini games?

Party games are group experiences where the phone goes from hand to hand — like taboo, silent word or who is the agent. Mini games are digital versions of classics such as tombala, trivia and nine men's morris. Both are in the same app.

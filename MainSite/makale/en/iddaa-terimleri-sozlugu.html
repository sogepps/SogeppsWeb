Betting Terms Dictionary: What Do Match Result, Over/Under, Both Teams to Score Mean?

Tahmin Meleği - İddaa 27 August 2026·2 min read

This content is for readers 18 and older. It is for informational and entertainment purposes; it is not advice about betting, alcohol consumption, or similar topics.

Abbreviations on football prediction slips look like code at first glance. The dictionary below explains with examples what the most common terms mean. This writing is only term explanation; it contains no betting advice.

Match Result (FT)

The result 90 minutes after the kickoff. Extra time and penalties are not included in this prediction.

  • FT 1 — The home team wins.
  • FT X — The match ends in a draw.
  • FT 2 — The away team wins.

Double Chance

An option that covers two of the three possible outcomes at once.

  • 1-X — Home wins or a draw.
  • X-2 — A draw or away wins.
  • 1-2 — Any result except a draw.

Over / Under (total goals)

Expresses whether the total goal count in a match will fall below or above a set limit. The most common limit is 2.5 goals.

  • 2.5 Over — The match has 3 or more goals.
  • 2.5 Under — The match has 0, 1, or 2 goals.
  • The reason the limit has a decimal is to make a tie impossible; you can't score 2.5 goals.

Both Teams to Score (BTTS)

  • BTTS Yes — Both teams score at least one goal.
  • BTTS No — At least one team fails to score.
  • In a 2-0 match, BTTS No happens; in a 1-1 match, BTTS Yes happens.

First Half / Match Result (HT-FT)

A prediction of both the first-half result and the final result together. "1-1" means the home team leads at half-time and also at the end, "X-2" means it's a draw at half-time but the away team wins the match.

Handicap

A prediction made by virtually giving one team a goal difference. When "Home −1 Handicap" is chosen, the home team needs to win by at least two goals. "+1 Handicap" makes the prediction valid even if the team loses by one goal.

Other common terms

  • Corner Over/Under: A prediction based on the total number of corners in a match.
  • Card Over/Under: A prediction based on the number of cards shown.
  • Odd/Even: Whether the total goal count will be odd or even.
  • First Goal Scorer: Which team will score the match's first goal.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does FT 1 mean?

It means the home team wins at the final whistle. The score 90 minutes after kickoff counts; extra time and penalties don't.

What does 2.5 Over mean?

It means a total of 3 or more goals are scored in the match. The decimal limit removes the possibility of a whole-number tie.

What does BTTS Yes mean?

It means both teams score at least one goal. It happens in scores like 1-1, 2-1, 3-2; it doesn't happen in scores like 2-0.

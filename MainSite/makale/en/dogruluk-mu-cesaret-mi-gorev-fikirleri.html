Truth or Dare? Party-Heating Task Ideas

Shot Çarkı 27 August 2026·2 min read

This content is for readers 18 and older. It is for informational and entertainment purposes; it is not advice about betting, alcohol consumption, or similar topics.

Truth or Dare is the easiest party game to set up; but without a good question list, it runs dry in three rounds. Below is a tiered list and a few rules that keep the game alive.

Truth questions — easy level

  • What's the most innocent lie you've kept hidden so far?
  • What are the three apps you use most on your phone?
  • Whose advice in this group do you trust the most?
  • Which TV show do you think is most overrated?
  • Did you regret a purchase you made in the last month?

Truth questions — medium level

  • Who in this group do you think you're most like?
  • If you could gain one skill overnight, what would it be?
  • When did you last need to apologize but didn't?
  • Is there a habit about yourself you'd like to change?

Dare tasks — easy level

  • Speak only in song lyrics for one minute.
  • Do an impersonation of someone in the group.
  • Describe your phone's last photo without looking at the gallery.
  • Stay straight-faced for one minute without laughing.

Dare tasks — hard level

  • Spend the next round standing on one leg.
  • Sing a 30-second improvised song to someone in the group.
  • Explain a movie using only facial expressions until someone gets it.
  • Start every sentence for the next two rounds with "in my opinion".

4 rules that keep the game alive

  • Leave room to pass. Everyone gets one pass; forcing the game kills it.
  • Don't pick the same person twice in a row. Turns should be randomly distributed.
  • Randomize the difficulty level. Hand-picking causes debate about favoritism.
  • Keep score. When there's a score, the game stays taken seriously even later in the night.

Letting a spinner decide turn order and difficulty

When people decide who plays and how hard the task is, debate breaks out. In Shot Spinner, the spinner sets both the turn order and difficulty level, picks a random task from the pool, and handles the score automatically. That way you focus on playing instead of managing the game.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many people play Truth or Dare?

You can play with a minimum of 3, but it's much more fun in groups of 5–10. In a large group, shorten round time to speed up waits.

How do you prevent uncomfortable questions?

Discuss boundaries at the start and give everyone a pass. Using a ready-made question pool prevents made-up cruel questions on the spot.

Is keeping score necessary?

Not necessary, but recommended. Games with a scoreboard keep getting attention later in the night.

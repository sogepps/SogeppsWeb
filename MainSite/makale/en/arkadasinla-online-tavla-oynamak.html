Play Backgammon Online with a Friend: Set Up a Private Table with an Invite Link

SogeGammon 27 August 2026·2 min read

When two people aren't in the same room, backgammon used to mean calling and shouting dice numbers. Today, all you need to do is send a link — when your opponent opens it, the table is already set.

How does the invite link work?

When you set up a private table, the game creates a special link for that table. You send this link to your friend through any messaging app. When they tap the link, they join your table directly; they don't go into a random matchmaking queue.

  • Create the table and pick the match length.
  • Copy the invite and send it to your friend.
  • When they tap the link, the match starts.
  • If they have the app installed, the link opens directly in the app.

Picking the right match length

In backgammon, match length sets the game's character. In short matches, a bad dice roll can end it; in long matches, skill difference shows.

  • 1 point (Lightning): Finishes in one hand, 5–10 minutes. For a quick round.
  • 5 points (Classic): Balanced; luck's effect drops, match runs about thirty minutes.
  • 10 points (Marathon): Long series; comebacks are possible, skill difference shows most clearly here.

No queue waiting: the difference of chip-free play

Many online backgammon games require chips to sit at a table; when your chips run out, you wait or pay. In a chip-free setup, losing doesn't kick you out — you can play ten matches in a row with your friend.

This makes a big difference especially in friend matches: playing series is much more fun than single games and gives a real answer to "who's better."

Switching between mobile and web

When you can log in from both browser and phone with the same account, it doesn't matter what device your friend is on. You can play from a computer, they from a phone; the invite link works the same way on both sides.

Small things that make the match more fun

  • In-game chat: trash-talking between moves is half the game.
  • Board theme: both players can pick their own look.
  • Dice effects and gifts: keeps the tempo up in long matches.
  • Friends list: once you add someone, sending an invite is one tap.

Where do you start?

After creating an account in SogeGammon, you can add your friend to the list or send an invite link directly. Since no chips are needed, you can play as many matches as you like, and join free tournaments in between.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play with a friend in a private table?

You create a private table and send the generated invite link to your friend. When they tap the link, they join your table directly, not a random matchmaking queue.

Does my friend have to download the app?

No. Since you can play from a browser too, your opponent can open the link and join the same match.

What match length should I pick?

1 point is good for a quick round, 5 points for a balanced match, and 10 points for a long series where skill difference becomes clear.

How To Play

Set up in a minute, then pass the phone around the table.

Set up the match

Pick the mode (classic or teams), the board (City or Adventure Island), dare categories and the number of rounds. Toggle extra rules like monsters.

Add the players

Add 2–10 players, type their names and pick a token from 12 characters. In team mode, split into Red and Blue.

Roll and do the dare

On your turn, roll the dice and do the dare on your tile. Done → the points are yours. Couldn’t do it → your friends will remember.

Climb the podium

When the rounds are over, the highest score wins. Podium, awards, player stats and a rematch.

Boards, Modes & Categories

Every match is set up the way your table likes it.

🏙️ City Board

The classic, simple board. Quick to learn, quick to play: perfect for a first game or a short session.

🏝️ Adventure Island ★

A 52-tile winding island with special tiles: Mini Game, Wheel of Fate, Duel, Everyone, Portal, Pit, Mud, Monster Lair, Golden (2×) and Flower Trap. Lap bonus at the finish arch.

🔴 Team Mode 🔵

Red team vs Blue team. Points are pooled, so every dare counts for the whole squad.

Dare categories

FamilySuitable for everyone, kids included.
PartyFunny, creative and embarrassing dares for friends.
Bold 18+Adults only. Pick what fits your crowd in match settings.

What’s New in Roll or Dare

The game was rebuilt from scratch. Here is what you get.

🎡 Wheel of Fate

+40, +25, +10, −10, Leader −15, Everyone +5, Roll Again or Joker. Spin and accept your fate.

⚔️ Duels

Challenge a rival to the same dare. The other players vote for the winner: +30 to the winner, +15 each on a draw.

👥 Everyone Dares

The whole table does the same dare at once. Your friends decide who did it best.

🎮 4 Mini Games

Tap Frenzy, Perfect Timing, Color Memory and Dice Guess for extra points.

🃏 Jokers & Golden Dares

Swap a dare with a joker, earn double points on golden tiles and grab streak bonuses for dares done in a row.

✍️ Your Own Dares

Write your own dares and add them to the deck. Inside jokes included.

⏱️ Timed Dares

Dares with a time limit get an automatic countdown right on the card.

🏆 Podium, Awards & Rematch

📊 Profiles, Stats & Daily Dare

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you download.

How many people can play Roll or Dare?

2 to 10 players on a single phone or tablet. Players take turns, so there is no need for extra devices.

Do I need an internet connection?

The game is played locally on one phone, but it requires an active internet connection to run, since it is free and supported by ads shown at the end of a match.

Is it suitable for kids and families?

Yes, with the Family category. Party and Bold (18+) categories contain dares meant for adults; you choose the categories in match settings before every game.

Can I add my own dares?

Yes. Open Your Own Dares from the main menu, write your dares and they join the deck in your next match.

Which languages are supported?

73 game languages, including English, Turkish, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Arabic, Hindi, Japanese and Korean. The game starts in your phone’s language and you can switch from the main menu.

How is it different from truth or dare?

It keeps the dares and adds a board game around them: dice, points, Adventure Island, monsters, mini games, the Wheel of Fate, duels and team mode. Every match has a winner on the podium.

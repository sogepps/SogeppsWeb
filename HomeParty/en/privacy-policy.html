🔐 Privacy Policy – HomeParty

Last updated: 23 June 2026

We at HomeParty ("App") value your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use the app, how it is used, and how it is protected.

By using the App, you accept this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

HomeParty does not require an account, login, or mandatory personal data such as your real name, email address, or phone number.

Data created during gameplay may include:

  • Default or custom player labels (e.g. Player1, Player2)
  • In-game scores, votes, and team assignments for the current session
  • Selected game mode, player count, and game settings
  • Short text entered in some modes (e.g. True or False statements)
  • Drawings created during a session in modes such as Draw Chain
  • Language and sound preferences

This information is stored only on your device to run the game. It is not uploaded to our servers. Word, question, and challenge prompts shown in the game are pre-written content for entertainment; the App does not access your phone history, contacts, photos, microphone, or other private device data.

2. Automatically Collected Information

To improve performance and stability, the following anonymous information may be collected automatically:

  • App usage and crash analytics
  • Device type and operating system version
  • General diagnostic data from the app platform

This data does not include player names, scores, votes, or custom text and drawings from your game sessions.

3. Advertising

HomeParty may use third-party advertising services (such as Google AdMob) to show ads. These providers may collect advertising identifiers and anonymous usage data to deliver and measure ads.

Ad providers are governed by their own privacy policies. You can limit personalized ads in your device settings (Google Ads settings on Android, or privacy settings on iOS).

4. Children's Privacy

HomeParty is a group party game that may be played by younger players as well. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. Some game modes (Hot Seat, Wheel Challenge, Most Likely, etc.) include social questions or challenges meant to be played verbally among players in the same room. Parents and guardians should supervise younger players.

5. Data Security

We design HomeParty to keep gameplay data on your device. We take reasonable measures to protect any data processed by the App.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. Uninstalling the app removes locally stored game data from your device.

6. Third-Party Services

The App may rely on third-party services (e.g. Google Mobile Ads, Unity analytics) with their own privacy practices. HomeParty is not responsible for how those services handle data. We recommend reviewing their policies.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page and take effect when published.

🔐 Privacy Policy – Sessiz Kelime

Last updated: 25 August 2026

As Sessiz Kelime ("App"), we care about user privacy. This policy explains the data usage approach of the mobile app.

1. Information We Collect

Sessiz Kelime does not require creating an account, logging in, or sharing mandatory personal data.

Data created during gameplay and possibly kept on the device may include:

  • Player / team names you enter for the session
  • Scores, round progress and game settings for the current session
  • Timer, pass allowance, category and language preferences
  • Sound / music / vibration settings

By default, this information is kept only on your device.

2. Automatically Collected Technical Information

Anonymous technical data may be collected to improve stability and performance:

  • Device type and operating system version
  • Crash / performance diagnostic logs
  • General usage statistics

This data does not include player names or game content.

3. Advertising and Third-Party Services

The mobile version may use advertising or analytics services (e.g. Google AdMob).

The data processing practices of these services are governed by their own privacy policies. You can limit personalized ads from your device settings.

4. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

5. Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are applied for data security. Local data may be deleted from the device when the app is uninstalled.

6. Policy Changes

Privacy policy updates are published on this page along with the date they take effect.

🔐 Privacy Policy – Neon Castles

Last updated: 5 October 2026

This policy explains how the Neon Castles mobile game ("the Game"), developed by Sogepps, handles data.

In short

  • The game shows ads: an occasional full-screen ad between levels (never in the first levels, and never for players who bought the No Ads Pack), plus fully optional rewarded videos you can choose to watch to double your reward, repair your castle or get free crystals.
  • Google AdMob, which serves the ads, may process information as a third party such as an advertising ID, your IP address, device/app interaction data (ad impressions and clicks) and diagnostics.
  • In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you're asked for consent through Google's consent form on first launch; you can change that choice any time in Settings → "Ad privacy options". Elsewhere, you can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android settings.
  • We (the developer) do not collect personal data ourselves; there's no account, sign-in or analytics of our own. Your game progress and settings stay only on your device.
  • Optional crystal packs and the No Ads Pack are processed entirely by Google Play; we never see your payment details.

1. We don't collect personal data

We (Sogepps), the developer of Neon Castles, do not collect your personal data, store it on our servers, or share it with anyone. There is no account or sign-in in the game, and we run no analytics of our own. The game shows ads; see section 3 below for what Google AdMob, as a third party, processes to serve them.

2. Information that stays on your device

Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device, in Android's local app storage. This information is never sent to us. It is deleted when you uninstall the game.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows two kinds of ads through Google AdMob: an occasional full-screen ad between levels (never shown in the first levels, and never to players who have bought the No Ads Pack), and fully optional rewarded videos you can choose to watch to double your reward, repair your castle or get free crystals. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google AdMob, as a third party, may process information such as:

  • Device identifiers such as the advertising ID
  • Your IP address
  • Device and app interaction data (ad impressions and clicks)
  • Diagnostics

How Google uses this data is described in Google's ad technology page and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Ad consent and your choices

Players in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown Google's consent form (UMP) on first launch and asked for their consent; you can change that choice any time under Settings → "Ad privacy options".

Players elsewhere can reset or delete their advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in their Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads / Privacy → Ads).

5. In-app purchases (crystal packs and the No Ads Pack)

You can optionally buy crystal packs and the No Ads Pack inside the game. These purchases are processed entirely by Google Play; we never see payment details such as card information. To deliver what you bought, the app only receives an anonymous purchase token from Google Play and keeps that token on your device to avoid delivering the same purchase twice.

Google's privacy policy applies to payments: policies.google.com/privacy.

6. Children

Neon Castles is not directed at children under 13.

7. Changes to this policy

When this policy is updated, the new text is published on this page.

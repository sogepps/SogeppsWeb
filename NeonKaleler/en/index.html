Features

⚔️ EPIC CASTLE BATTLES

Two castles stand at the ends of the battlefield. Collect gold, train troops and attack wave after wave. Let your swordsmen clash on the front line while archers rain arrows from behind and mages blow enemy crowds sky-high!

⛏️ RUN YOUR MINES

The miners behind your castle haul gold non-stop. Hire more miners, grow your economy and field a bigger army than your enemy. The commander who invests at the right time wins!

🛡️ 16 UNIQUE UNITS

Swordsman, Archer, Spearman, Shieldbearer, Cavalry, Mage, Healer, Ninja, Bomber, Catapult, Giant, Sharpshooter, Dragon, Necromancer, Paladin and the legendary Mecha Titan! Each one has its own strength. Find the right combination and build the best strategy against every enemy.

☄️ DEVASTATING SPELLS

Call down meteors, heal your army, send your troops into a rage, freeze your enemies or strike them with lightning. One spell at the right moment can turn the whole battle!

👹 GIANT BOSSES

A giant boss awaits you every 10 levels, and the lord of each realm waits at its end. Are you ready to face giants, dragons and colossal war machines?

🌍 300 LEVELS, 10 WORLDS

From Neon Valley to Crimson Desert, from Frost Realm to Lava Forge, from Cyber City to the Void Throne: 10 different worlds. Special levels like Raid, Swarm, Siege and Air Strike make every battle different.

📈 ENDLESS PROGRESSION

Use the crystals you win to power up every unit up to level 30. Raise your castle walls, grow your treasury, and boost your castle archers and spell power. Even after a defeat, your crystals stay with you!

✨ VIBRANT NEON GRAPHICS

Glowing neon lights, explosions, battle effects and immersive synthwave music for a stunning war experience.

🎁 A GIFT EVERY DAY

Log in every day, claim your daily gift and grow your army faster.

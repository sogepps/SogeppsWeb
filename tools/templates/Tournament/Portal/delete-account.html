Delete Account

Sign in with your email and password, then type the confirmation phrase to permanently delete your account and all associated data.

What will be deleted
  • Account profile information
  • Authentication credentials linked to the account
  • User-created tournament and league records
  • Any user-associated app preferences and settings
Data retention

All personal data is permanently deleted after account deletion. No personal data is retained. We do not keep any copy of your data; there is no retention period after deletion.

Deletion timing

Deletion is processed immediately after successful confirmation. Your account and data are removed as soon as you complete the form.

You must type exactly I confirm (in English, case-insensitive). This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

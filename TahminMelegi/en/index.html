How Tahmin Meleği Works

One phone, one ritual: think, rub, read.

1) The witch appears

The storm breaks, the witch arrives and whispers: think of a match and rub the cauldron.

2) Rub the cauldron

Rub the fate cauldron with your finger. It boils, lightning speaks.

3) The parchment opens

When the cauldron speaks, a parchment rises and the prophecy is written.

4) Read one pick

A single random sign from match result, over/under, BTTS and other markets.

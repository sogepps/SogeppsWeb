🔐 Privacy Policy – Tahmin Meleği - İddaa

Last updated: 19 August 2026

We at Tahmin Meleği - İddaa ("App") value your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use the app, how it is used, and how it is protected.

By using the App, you accept this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Tahmin Meleği - İddaa does not require an account, login, or mandatory personal data such as your real name, email address, or phone number.

Data created while using the App may include:

  • Random prophecy text generated on your device during a ritual
  • In-session game state such as sound and haptic feedback
  • Language preference, if any

This information is stored only on your device to run the App. It is not uploaded to our servers. You do not need to type team names; the match you think of stays in your mind. The App does not access your contacts, photos, browser history, or other private device data.

2. Automatically Collected Information

To improve performance and stability, the following anonymous information may be collected automatically:

  • App usage and crash analytics
  • Device type and operating system version
  • General diagnostic data from the app platform

This data does not include the match you hold in mind or the prophecy text from your session.

3. Advertising

Tahmin Meleği - İddaa may use third-party advertising services (such as Google AdMob) to show interstitial ads between rituals. These providers may collect advertising identifiers and anonymous usage data to deliver and measure ads. The App may request non-personalized ads where supported.

Ad providers are governed by their own privacy policies. You can limit personalized ads in your device settings.

4. Children's Privacy & Responsible Use

Tahmin Meleği - İddaa is an entertainment app with football betting-themed random predictions. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. Prophecies are random and are not betting advice. The App is not intended for users under 18. Parents and guardians should supervise younger users.

5. Data Security

We design Tahmin Meleği - İddaa to keep gameplay data on your device. We take reasonable measures to protect any data processed by the App.

No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. Uninstalling the app removes locally stored data from your device.
